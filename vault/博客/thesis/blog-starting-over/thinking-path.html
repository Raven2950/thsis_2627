01
Turning pointA realization

A lot of what I made was what I felt I should make

Practical, employable, and something I neither hated nor loved. My attitude to most things had been “whatever.” I decided to throw everything out and start over.

“What do I actually like?”

so I turned the research on myself

then I asked why two games pull me in

03
Exploration 2Dissecting references

It isn’t their worlds that draw me in. It’s a shared temperament

The hero is already broken before the story starts
The world comes after a revolution or rebellion has failed
There’s more than one voice in your head
Body and mind are slowly used up
Lore comes in fragments you piece together
Moments of grace in the middle of decay

Disco Elysium turns inward (the mind is the world); Cyberpunk 2077 turns outward (the city and the body are the world). I built a library of about 80 works and sources, sorted by those five threads: novels, films, games, philosophy, and the history of cyberpunk.

which changed how I see my own project

04
ReframeWhat I decided

If I copied a world like theirs, it would end up ungrounded and not genuine

Form / texture ← the referencesI study them to learn how they create the feeling. My keywords describe a mood, not a plot.
Content / story ← my own lifeA smaller story that I personally feel connected to.

Medium: a complete, story-driven game by May (Unity, in the spirit of Disco Elysium, but small and deep). It’s a medium I know, it carries both images and text, and I love writing.

which leaves one open question
