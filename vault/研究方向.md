### MY key areas of interest
At this early stage, I am not trying to define one specific thesis topic. Instead, I am mapping out a possible space for a practical, user-centred project.
My main areas of interest are AI-supported software, web tools, inclusive UX, and assistive technology. I am particularly interested in projects that can be researched with real users and developed into a working digital prototype.
Accessibility is currently an important area for me because my course provides the opportunity to collaborate with disability organizations and communicate directly with disabled users. This makes the area more researchable and allows me to avoid designing only from assumptions.
In terms of medium, I prefer software-based or digital-first projects, such as web applications, AI-powered tools, interactive prototypes, or digital services. I am less interested in projects that depend heavily on fabrication, expensive hardware, or complex mechanical systems. However, I am open to small physical components if they are necessary to support the user experience.
I am also interested in using AI where it can solve a real user problem, rather than using AI only because it is technically fashionable. Possible areas include personalized assistance, communication support, information access, daily task support, and accessible interaction. At this stage, these are possibilities rather than final research directions.
For my thesis, I want to find a problem that is realistic to investigate, allows meaningful user research, can be prototyped within the available time and resources, and has potential value as a portfolio project or future product. My next step is therefore to research different accessibility-related needs and existing digital tools, speak with potential users or partner organizations, and identify which problems are both meaningful and feasible to explore.

Thesis Research Repository
1. Repository Overview

This repository collects early research and inspiration related to my possible thesis directions.
At this stage, I am not focusing on one specific research question. Instead, I am exploring the intersection of:
- Accessibility and inclusive design
- AI-supported software
- Web applications and digital tools

- User-centred product design

The purpose of this repository is to collect:
- Articles and guidelines
- Existing digital products
- Assistive technologies
- AI-powered tools
- Design projects
- Interaction patterns
- User problems
- Potential research methods
I will use this repository to compare different opportunities and gradually identify a feasible thesis direction.

2. Current Research Criteria
When evaluating a possible thesis direction, I will consider:
1. Is there a real user problem?
1. Can I access or recruit relevant users?
1. Can I conduct meaningful user research?
1. Could the problem be addressed through software or a web-based tool?
1. Is AI genuinely useful for the problem?
1. Can I create and test a prototype within the available time?
1. Does the project avoid unnecessary hardware or fabrication?
1. Could the final project become a strong portfolio case study?
1. Does the project have potential real-world or commercial value?
1. Can I continue developing the project after the thesis?

### 四个方向的出发点

**Assistive Technology**
I want to investigate how assistive technology supports independence, communication, information access, and participation.

I am particularly interested in software-based or digital-first assistive products because they are more feasible for my project than hardware-heavy solutions.

**AI and Human-Centred Products**
I am interested in AI as a possible component of a practical digital product. At this stage, I do not want to use AI simply because it is fashionable. I want to investigate when AI can provide meaningful assistance and when it may create new problems.
Possible areas include:
- Personalised assistance
- Information access
- Text simplification
- Voice and speech interfaces
- Image or environment description
- Task support
- Recommendation systems
- Adaptive interfaces
- User control and transparency

**Accessibility and Inclusive Design**

I want to understand how accessibility is approached in digital products and services, including visual, auditory, motor, cognitive, communication, and neurodiversity-related needs.
I am also interested in the difference between designing for accessibility as a technical requirement and designing with disabled people as active participants in the research and design process.
