Type:
AAC education and advocacy organisation

Focus:
Augmentative and Alternative Communication (AAC)

Why it is relevant:
AAC includes many different ways of communicating when speech alone does not meet a person’s needs.

Topics to investigate:
- Communication is not limited to speech.
- AAC can include low-tech and high-tech systems.
- Users may need different communication methods in different environments.
- The tool is only one part of communication; communication partners and social context also matter.

Possible thesis connection:
If I explore communication technology, I need to understand the user’s whole communication environment instead of designing only a text-generation feature.
