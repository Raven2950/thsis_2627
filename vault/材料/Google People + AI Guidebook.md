> **一句话**：Google PAIR 团队的 AI 产品设计指南，讲 AI 不确定、会出错时，产品该怎么和用户沟通。
> 
> **建议怎么读**：只读一章：Explainability + Trust，或者 Feedback + Control（约 15 分钟）。
> 
> **读的时候留意**
> - 什么该自动做、什么留给用户控制（Q5）
> - AI 出错时怎么让用户察觉、纠正
> - 怎么告诉用户 AI 有多不确定
> 
> **可以摘录的方向**：每章开头的要点小结，再配上你自己的一个应用场景。

> [!note] Update 1 笔记（从 Notion 导入）
> Link: [Google People + AI Guidebook](https://pair.withgoogle.com/guidebook-v2/)
> Type:
> AI product design guidebook
>
> Main topic:
> Human-centred design for AI products
>
> Why I collected it:
> This resource focuses on designing AI systems from a user and product perspective, rather than only focusing on the technology.
>
> What I learned:
> AI products have special UX challenges because the system may be uncertain, unpredictable, or difficult for users to understand.
>
> Potential connection to my thesis:
> This could help me think about how a future AI product should communicate its limitations, ask for user input, provide feedback, and allow users to correct the system.
>
> Questions:
> - What should the AI do automatically?
> - What should remain under the user’s control?
> - How should the product communicate uncertainty?
> - How can users correct or customise the AI?
> - What happens when the AI makes a wrong assumption?
